About

I work at Viettel Cyber Security and graduated in Computer Science from Hanoi University of Science and Technology, where I do research at the AI4LIFE Research Center under Assoc. Prof. Dr. Nguyễn Phi Lê.

My work sits across Natural Language Processing, data design and Computer Vision. I work the full ML lifecycle: designing datasets, collecting and cleaning data, feature engineering, then training, fine-tuning, evaluating and deploying models.

I’m planning to pursue a Master’s degree to go deeper into AI, and I’m always keen to join collaborative projects that turn research into something people actually use.

Off the keyboard: reading research, travelling, learning languages and solving puzzles.
